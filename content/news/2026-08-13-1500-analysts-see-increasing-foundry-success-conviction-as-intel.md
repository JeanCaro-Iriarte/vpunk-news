Analysts are optimistic about Intel's future growth, citing accelerated progress and increased capital expenditures in its foundry division. The company's CEO, Lip-Bu Ran, has added a significant amount of his own money to the stock in an apparent move to boost confidence.

This development comes as analysts note that Intel is investing heavily in external customers with its foundry operations. In addition, the company has expanded capex (capital expenditure) to support this growth strategy.

The move by CEO Ran has been interpreted by some as a signal of increased conviction about landing major external customer contracts.
