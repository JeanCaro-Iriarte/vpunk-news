The Bank of England (BoE) has conducted a test of its digital currency and stablecoin in the trade finance sector. The test aimed to assess the potential impact on global SMEs, with an emphasis on enhancing efficiency and reducing costs.

The tests involved the creation and use of digital versions of traditional fiat currencies and stablecoins in international trade transactions. This move is seen as a key step towards developing a decentralized financial system that could increase participation and usage of alternative payment methods.

By testing its new digital currency and stablecoin, the BoE hopes to improve the efficiency and reducing costs for SMEs participating in global trade. This shift could also enable increased competition and innovation in the financial industry, which may have far-reaching consequences for the global economy.
