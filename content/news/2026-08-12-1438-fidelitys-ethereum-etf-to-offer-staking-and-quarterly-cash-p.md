Fidelity has announced it will launch an Ethereum Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF), which will allow investors to purchase and trade Ether (ETH) on a blockchain-based platform. The fund, titled Fidelity Ethereum Trust, will offer two key benefits: staking, where investors can stake their holdings for rewards in the form of quarterly cash payouts.

In addition to staking, the ETF will also offer a unique feature that is set in conjunction with BlackRock's own Ethereum ETF launch. According to reports, Fidelity's fund will permit investors to stake their assets directly within the platform, providing faster and potentially more efficient execution compared to traditional staking methods.

Fidelity's Ethereum Trust is expected to be available for trading starting in early 2024, although a specific date has not been confirmed. This move comes as investors seek innovative ways to participate in the growing Ethereum ecosystem and as institutional players increase their involvement in digital assets.
